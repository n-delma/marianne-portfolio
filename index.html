Hi, I'm Marianne — I make things that feel like something.

I'm a multimedia artist and illustrator based in Batangas, Philippines. My work moves between illustration, editorial cartooning, motion, and photography — but the thread is always the same: making images that carry weight.

I've spent years building visual narratives for The Brahmans' Journal as artist, photojournalist, and senior artist. These days, I'm finishing my degree in Multimedia Arts at the University of Batangas — Lipa City.

How a piece becomes a piece.

01

Sit with the feeling

Every piece begins with an emotion or a memory I can't shake. I let it linger before I sketch.

02

Find the symbol

I look for an image — a hand, a moth, a window — that carries what words can't.

03

Build it slowly

Sketches, layers, color tests. I work between Procreate and Photoshop until the image breathes.

04

Let it leave

Final pass, export, share. The piece belongs to whoever finds something of themselves in it.
